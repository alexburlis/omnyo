# Workdesk

- **Actions:** what needs you, across projects.
- **Approve:** send the request to its chat.
- **Decline:** dismiss silently.
- **Work:** short outcomes with evidence and source links.

Cards, counts and the drawer refresh together. Full notes stay expandable.
Records survive archival and plugin reloads. No external services or extra AI calls.

Agents: read `omnyo workdesk context`, report concise bullets, resolve completed actions,
and checkpoint before handing back. See the bundled Workdesk skill.
