## Cross-package checks

- A read-only `git ls-tree -r -z HEAD apps packages plugins scripts tools` pass grouped regular TS, TSX, JS and MJS blobs by object ID. Counting groups of at least two files and at least 15 newline-delimited lines gave 32 groups and 5,926 duplicate lines. The 14 groups with `.test.` or `.fixture.` in their names account for 3,840 lines. The remaining 18 groups account for 2,086 lines. This is a candidate detector. The shared ACP coverage and other use sites determine what can be removed.
- Traced live consumers for the rename maps, lab routes, public catalog, generated asset manifest, Node/browser SDK and the demo server before proposing deletion or consolidation. Scoped searches did not establish absence of third-party imports or unpublished operational use. No generic unused-file or dependency result was used as deletion evidence.
- Kept public traffic admission and transport rules, demo fixture handling, developer preview URLs, test fixtures with active callers and legacy user-state readers where callers depend on them. Size or textual similarity alone did not justify a common implementation.
